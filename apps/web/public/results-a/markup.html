Questions · Answered

Ask us
anything.

Anything we haven’t covered, ask at your consultation. It’s free, and there’s no such thing as a silly question about your own teeth.

How do I know if aligners are right for me?

Book a free consultation. A dentist takes a 3D scan in person and tells you honestly whether aligners will work for you. If they won’t, you pay nothing.

How long does treatment take?

Most Moves take four to six months. Your exact timeline is shown on your plan, tray by tray, before you pay a pound.

Do I need clinic appointments?

You’re examined, scanned and fitted in person at the start. After that, your aligners are delivered to your door and check-ins reach you before you have to ask.

Do they hurt?

Expect mild pressure for a day or two each time you move to a new tray. That’s the teeth moving. It settles quickly; most people adjust within a week.

What do I actually have to do?

Wear each set around 22 hours a day, take them out to eat and clean your teeth, and swap to the next on schedule. We check in along the way.

Is whitening really included?

Yes. Whitening and retainers are in every Move, at every price. No add-ons, no upsell at the end.

How do I start?

Book a free consultation. Scan day takes about twenty minutes in person. That’s the whole first move.
